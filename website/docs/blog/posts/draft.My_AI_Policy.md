TL;DR: I use LLMs as team members, review all outputs carefully, and reject AI slop.

<!-- more -->

- I use LLMs in the same way I work with people on my team

- I use LLMs to fix my English (sorry after 25 years, sometimes I still prefer to
  write in Italian and getting it translated)

- I review every single line of code / output from an LLM multiple times
  in the same way I review PRs from junior team members that are smart, but not
  trustworthy

- I abhor AI slop and anything that looks, tastes, feels like that
