TL;DR: I use LLMs as team members, review all outputs carefully, and despise AI slop

<!-- more -->

- I use LLMs in the same way I work with humans on my team

- I use LLMs to fix my English
  - After 25+ years, sometimes I still prefer to write in Italian and getting it
    translated

- I review every single line of code / output from an LLM multiple times
  in the same way I review PRs from junior team members that are smart, but not
  trustworthy

- I need to be able to explain everything in my projects
  - Every line of code, every comment, every design decision
  - If I can't do that, I'm using AI improperly

- Results produced with AI should be of higher quality than results without it
  - AI is a tool to enhance my work, not replace my critical thinking

- I watch out for becoming dependent on AI
  - If I can't work without it (e.g., write code, think through problems, debug
    issues), that's a red flag
  - AI should augment my capabilities, not let me bypass learning or problem-solving

- I use AI for brainstorming, but exercise critical judgment
  - I always verify AI suggestions for accuracy
  - I watch out against hallucinations
  - I challenge outputs and make informed choices
  - I don't let the machine take over my decision-making or give out to AI-lazyiness

- I abhor AI slop and anything that looks, tastes, feels like that
  - AI;DR: If I feel something is AI generated, I lose interest

- My peers will review my work, so I am ready to justify my use of AI and defend
  every part of what I've created
