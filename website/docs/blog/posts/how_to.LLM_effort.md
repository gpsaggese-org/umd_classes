# Reasoning Effort and Cost Across AI Models

## Pricing

- Most AI providers charge based on token usage:

  | Token Type | Description |
  |------------|-------------|
  | Input | Tokens sent to the model |
  | Output | Tokens generated by the model |

- The per-token price is determined by the model and provider.

## What Higher Reasoning Effort Changes

- Many models offer settings such as:
  - Low effort
  - Medium effort
  - High effort
  - Extended thinking
  - Deep reasoning

- Higher reasoning settings typically cause the model to:
  - Spend more compute on reasoning
  - Generate longer responses
  - Use more reasoning and output tokens
  - Take longer to respond

## Cost Impact

The impact depends on the provider:

### Scenario 1: Token-Based Billing

The per-token price stays the same, but total cost increases because the model uses more tokens.

| Effort Level | Typical Token Usage | Relative Cost |
|-------------|--------------------|---------------|
| Low | Baseline | 1× |
| Medium | Higher | 1.2–2× |
| High | Much higher | 2–5×+ |

### Scenario 2: Fixed-Priced Reasoning Tiers

Some providers may charge different rates for different reasoning modes or model variants.

In this case, both:
- Token usage may increase
- Per-token pricing may increase

## Rule of Thumb

- **Lower effort** → Faster, cheaper, sufficient for routine tasks
- **Higher effort** → Better reasoning quality, slower responses, higher cost
- **Cost depends on both pricing and total token consumption**

Always check the provider's pricing documentation, as the relationship between reasoning effort and billing varies by platform and model.
