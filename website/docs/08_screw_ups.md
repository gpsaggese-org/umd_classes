# My Top Mistakes

## Summary

- A running list of my biggest mistakes
- to keep the ego in check and shows how difficult it is to make the right decisions
- Ex-post, my general strategy for becoming successful is apparently: be very close
  to extremely good decisions, then do the opposite at the last second

### 1999 - Refused Berkeley
- I got an offer to do my PhD at Berkeley in 1999, but my mother (and my girlfriend
  of the time) convinced me to stay in Italy and help Italy come back
  - I would have been in Silicon Valley in the midst of the DotCom bubble
  - Can you even image it?

- I ended up still studying in the US, but in the depression of post-Dot Com bubble
  in the snowy Midwest
  - I got rid of that girlfriend (not of my mother)

### 2004 - Crypto Hardware Paper

- Wrote a paper on how to use hardware to accelerate cryptographic primitives (SHA1
  and Merkle Trees, which are exactly what hardware cryptomining does)
- Years later, cryptominers told me the ideas in my paper were used in several crypto
  mining hardware systems

### 2005 - Declined Google

- Declined an offer from Google (when the interviews were almost impossible to pass!)
- Larry Page called me in person to tell me that I was the first person in Google
  history not to accept an offer

### 2007 - Elon Musk and Tesla

- Met Elon Musk at a barbecue in Palo Alto (he was surprisingly tall and it was
  before billions of dollars made his hair grow back)
- Over burgers he told me about Tesla. I then tried to convince him that big oil
  companies would never allow Tesla to succeed

- I had a second chance on making money how to Tesla
  - I bought one of the first Model S in ~2012 (people would stop me in the middle of
    the street to take pictures of the inside! It was that rare to see it in the
    street)
  - Still managed to not buy Tesla at IPO

### 2007 - Sold NVIDIA Too Early

- Sold a (very) large amount of NVIDIA stock that would have ended up being roughly a
  1000x gain

### 2008 - Declined Facebook

- Interviewed at Facebook. At the question "What is your favorite Facebook
  feature?" I answered with a "I don't have a Facebook account. Social networks
  are clearly a fad"
- Despite this aweful answer, I still got an offer
- When I didn't take it, they offered me money to talk to their product team to
  study why I could be resistant to the addiction
- As of right now, I still don't have a Facebook and Instagram account

### 2011 - Declined Uber

- My next door neighbor in San Jose was an early employee at Uber
- So he took me out for coffee and proposed me to join Uber working on AI
- I told him that I didn’t want to join a taxi company
- I would have been around employee ~20

### 2012 - Didn’t Buy Bitcoin

- Almost bought $100k worth of Bitcoin at around $1 on Mount Gox, but then got
  distracted by something and forgot to push "buy"
- Then I let other people convince me that Bitcoin was nonsense (thank you Will!)

For the next batch of mistakes I need to let some time pass by to see how things
shake down. Stay tuned!
