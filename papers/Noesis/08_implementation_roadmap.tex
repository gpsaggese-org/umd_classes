\section{Implementation roadmap}
\label{sec:roadmap}

The implementation plans in the underlying design notes for
\NoesisMarket{} and \NoesisServer{} were written independently; this
section merges them into a single sequence in which each milestone is a
result that can be demonstrated on its own, and later milestones wire
earlier ones together into the closed loop described in
Section~\ref{sec:protocol_overview}.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Milestone 1: a minimal batch call-auction simulator.} An
    in-memory order book with bid/ask submission and per-tier
    uniform-price clearing, implementing Problem~\ref{prob:clearing} for
    the complete-compatibility-graph case of
    Proposition~\ref{prop:existence}. Result: a unit-tested auction that
    clears correctly on synthetic order books, with no real settlement.

  \item \textbf{Milestone 2: a minimal passthrough proxy with logging.}
    A gateway supporting two or three providers behind one API,
    implementing the logging schema of
    Section~\ref{sec:gateway_architecture}. Result: every request/response
    pair is logged and queryable, independent of Milestone 1.

  \item \textbf{Milestone 3: contract schema and dispatch integration.}
    Wire Milestone 1's cleared bids to Milestone 2's proxy: define the
    contract schema of Definition~\ref{def:contract} in code, and hand
    off a cleared contract from \NoesisMarket{} to \NoesisServer{} for
    execution. Result: a closed loop in which a matched contract is
    dispatched and executed, though not yet monitored for fulfillment.

  \item \textbf{Milestone 4: a routing experiment.} Add a simple routing
    policy (e.g., a task classifier standing in for
    $\hat d$ in Equation~\eqref{eq:difficulty_estimator}) to the proxy
    from Milestone 2, and measure cost against quality relative to
    always calling the strongest model. Result: a measured cost/quality
    frontier for at least one routing policy.

  \item \textbf{Milestone 5: fulfillment monitoring and the reputation
    feedback loop.} Implement the measured-reliability computation of
    Equation~\eqref{eq:measured_reliability} and the violation test of
    Equation~\eqref{eq:reliability_lower_bound} in \NoesisServer, and
    the reputation update of Equation~\eqref{eq:reputation_update} in
    \NoesisMarket. Result: a contract dispatched under Milestone 3 has
    its fulfillment measured, and a seller that under-delivers is priced
    out or excluded from subsequent rounds.

  \item \textbf{Milestone 6: a difficulty estimator and a first
    distillation experiment.} Train $\hat d$ from the logged agreement
    label of Definition~\ref{def:agreement_label}, evaluate the saving
    condition of Equation~\eqref{eq:routing_saving_condition} on real
    traffic, and run a first distillation experiment on the logged
    corpus (Section~\ref{sec:distillation}). Result: a difficulty-aware
    router with a measured saving, and a first distilled model.

  \item \textbf{Milestone 7: the full closed loop.} With Milestones 1
    through 6 in place, run \NoesisMarket{} and \NoesisServer{} together
    end to end: bids and asks clear into contracts, contracts are
    executed and monitored, fulfillment results feed back into pricing
    and eligibility, and routing and fusion decisions are informed by
    the accumulating logged corpus. Result: a working prototype
    exercising every mechanism described in
    Sections~\ref{sec:noesis_market} and \ref{sec:noesis_server}.
\end{itemize}

Each milestone is scoped to be independently demonstrable within one to
two days of focused work, consistent with the goal of showing progress
incrementally rather than attempting the full closed loop in one step.
