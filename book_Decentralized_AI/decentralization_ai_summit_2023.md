# Future of Decentralization, AI, and Computing Summit

Source: https://rdi.berkeley.edu/events/decentralizationaisummit

## Date & Location

- **Date**: August 27, 2023
- **Venue**: Pauley Ballroom, Martin Luther King Jr. Building, UC Berkeley

## Description

The summit brought together researchers and innovators to examine how
artificial intelligence and decentralization technology can advance each
other. Key focus areas included decentralized ML infrastructure such as
decentralized training, inference, provenance, and integrity guarantees,
along with open-source data and models, privacy-preserving personalization,
and governance frameworks for responsible AI development.
