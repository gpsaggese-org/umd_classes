# Decentralized Finance Course Topics

- **Blockchain and consensus foundations**
  - UTXO vs account models
  - Proof-of-work vs proof-of-stake
  - Finality, chain reorganizations

- **Smart contract architecture and security**
  - EVM execution model
  - Reentrancy
  - Oracle manipulation
  - Formal verification

- **Automated market makers (AMMs)**
  - Constant-product/constant-sum curves
  - Concentrated liquidity
  - Impermanent loss

- **Decentralized exchanges and order flow**
  - Order-book DEXs, aggregators
  - MEV-aware routing
  - Slippage

- **Lending and borrowing protocols**
  - Overcollateralization
  - Interest-rate models
  - Liquidation mechanics
  - Flash loans

- **Stablecoins and monetary design**
  - Fiat-collateralized, crypto-backed, algorithmic pegs
  - Depeg risk (Terra/UST case study)

- **Derivatives and synthetic assets**
  - Perpetual futures
  - Options protocols
  - Synthetic exposure
  - Funding rates

- **MEV and transaction ordering**
  - Sandwich attacks
  - Front-running
  - PBS, proposer-builder separation
  - MEV redistribution

- **Oracle design and data integrity**
  - Chainlink-style feeds
  - TWAPs
  - Oracle manipulation attacks
  - Latency/trust tradeoffs

- **Governance and DAOs**
  - Token-weighted voting
  - Delegation
  - Treasury management
  - Governance attack vectors

- **Tokenomics and incentive design**
  - Emission schedules
  - ve-token models
  - Liquidity mining
  - Protocol-owned liquidity

- **Cross-chain interoperability and bridges**
  - Bridge trust models
  - Wrapped assets
  - Bridge exploits
  - Rollup-native messaging

- **Layer 2 scaling for DeFi**
  - Optimistic/zk-rollups
  - State channels
  - Fee markets
  - Composability across layers

- **Risk management and systemic risk**
  - Protocol composability ("money legos")
  - Contagion
  - Collateral cascades
  - Stress testing

- **Regulation and compliance**
  - Securities classification
  - AML/KYC in permissionless systems
  - MiCA/DeFi-adjacent frameworks
