# Goal
- Given the research ideas come up with 5 new ideas that are thought provoking,
  worth of research along the lines of the current research ideas

# Workflow

## Read Research Ideas
- Read `research/ideas/README.md`
- Read the template for a research idea `research/ideas/template.research_idea.md`
- Read the research ideas under `research/ideas/*.md`

## Conventions
- When writing follow the conventions in `.claude/skills/markdown.rules.md` and
  `.claude/skills/text.rules.md`
- Fit the text in 80-90 character lines
- The template is `research/ideas/template.research_idea.md`

## Come Up with 5 Ideas
- Given the research ideas come up with 5 new ideas that are thought provoking,
  worth of research along the lines of the current research ideas
