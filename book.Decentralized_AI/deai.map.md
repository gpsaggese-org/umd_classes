# Decentralized AI Course Topics

15 topics for advanced college course on decentralized artificial intelligence.

1. **Federated learning** — FedAvg aggregation, personalization, non-IID data,
   communication overhead.
2. **Decentralized training** — data/model parallelism across untrusted
   nodes, communication-efficient SGD, bandwidth limits.
3. **Decentralized compute & inference markets** — Bittensor, Gensyn,
   Together; incentive design for compute-sharing networks.
4. **Blockchain-AI integration** — on-chain agents, smart-contract-triggered
   inference, oracles feeding ML outputs on-chain.
5. **Zero-knowledge ML (ZKML)** — zk-SNARKs for verifiable inference,
   proof-of-training, trustless model correctness.
6. **Differential privacy** — DP-SGD, privacy budgets, accuracy tradeoffs in
   decentralized settings.
7. **Secure multiparty computation & homomorphic encryption** —
   privacy-preserving collaborative training/inference.
8. **Model & data provenance** — watermarking, dataset lineage,
   tamper-evident training records.
9. **Decentralized data marketplaces** — data DAOs, tokenized datasets,
   incentive-compatible data sharing.
10. **AI governance via DAOs** — voting on model updates, compute treasury
    management, governance attack vectors.
11. **Multi-agent systems & mechanism design** — cooperative/competitive
    learning, incentive alignment, self-play economics.
12. **Open-source vs closed-source ecosystems** — licensing, trust,
    evaluation (e.g. DecodingTrust), reproducibility.
13. **Robustness & security** — poisoning attacks, Sybil resistance,
    adversarial nodes in federated/decentralized systems.
14. **Tokenized incentive design for AI networks** — reward mechanisms for
    compute/data contributors, ve-token-style models.
15. **Regulation & responsible AI** — accountability vs decentralization
    tension, AI Act/MiCA-adjacent frameworks, alignment with
    `responsible_genai_f23.md`.
