# Decentralized Finance Course Topics

15 topics for advanced college course on decentralized finance.

1. **Blockchain & consensus foundations** — UTXO vs account models,
   proof-of-work vs proof-of-stake, finality, chain reorganizations.
2. **Smart contract architecture & security** — EVM execution model,
   reentrancy, oracle manipulation, formal verification.
3. **Automated market makers (AMMs)** — constant-product/constant-sum
   curves, concentrated liquidity, impermanent loss.
4. **Decentralized exchanges & order flow** — order-book DEXs, aggregators,
   MEV-aware routing, slippage.
5. **Lending & borrowing protocols** — overcollateralization, interest-rate
   models, liquidation mechanics, flash loans.
6. **Stablecoins & monetary design** — fiat-collateralized, crypto-backed,
   algorithmic pegs, depeg risk (Terra/UST case study).
7. **Derivatives & synthetic assets** — perpetual futures, options
   protocols, synthetic exposure, funding rates.
8. **MEV & transaction ordering** — sandwich attacks, front-running,
   PBS, proposer-builder separation, MEV redistribution.
9. **Oracle design & data integrity** — Chainlink-style feeds, TWAPs,
   oracle manipulation attacks, latency/trust tradeoffs.
10. **Governance & DAOs** — token-weighted voting, delegation, treasury
    management, governance attack vectors.
11. **Tokenomics & incentive design** — emission schedules, ve-token
    models, liquidity mining, protocol-owned liquidity.
12. **Cross-chain interoperability & bridges** — bridge trust models,
    wrapped assets, bridge exploits, rollup-native messaging.
13. **Layer 2 scaling for DeFi** — optimistic/zk-rollups, state channels,
    fee markets, composability across layers.
14. **Risk management & systemic risk** — protocol composability
    ("money legos"), contagion, collateral cascades, stress testing.
15. **Regulation & compliance** — securities classification, AML/KYC in
    permissionless systems, MiCA/DeFi-adjacent frameworks.
